Not sure? How to check

Open myAadhaar on the UIDAI website → Bank seeding status, or ask at your bank branch with your passbook. If it says “Inactive”, the scholarship can’t be paid into it.

What is a Jan Dhan account?

A zero-balance account opened under the Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana. Some have an overdraft (credit) limit — scholarships can’t be paid into those. Your passbook or bank will tell you.
